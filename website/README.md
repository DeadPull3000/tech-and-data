# Website Project Workspace

Welcome to the **Website** project workspace within the Tech & Data vertical of the **Quizzing Club**.

## Purpose

This workspace is dedicated to developing and maintaining the Quizzing Club's official web presence. The website will eventually serve as the digital face of the club, showcasing:

- **Club Activities & Events**: Details about weekly practice sessions, inter-college quizzes, workshops, and leagues.
- **Upcoming Quizzes & Schedules**: Interactive calendars and registration links for upcoming quizzing events.
- **Announcements & News**: Official club updates, member spotlights, and recaps.
- **Achievements & Leaderboards**: Hall of fame, past tournament winners, and seasonal standings.
- **Resources & Archives**: Reference question archives, study material, and guides for aspiring quizmasters.

## Technical Approach & Guardrails

To ensure that the club arrives at the right architecture collaboratively:

- **No Premature Implementation**: We have intentionally refrained from implementing the website or choosing a specific frontend framework, static site generator, or CMS at this stage.
- **Unopinionated Workspace**: This keeps the repository clean and lightweight while technical discussions take place.
- **Ready for Scaffolding**: Once requirements are finalized through discussion, the chosen application can be initialized cleanly directly inside this directory.

## How to Propose & Build

1. **Share Ideas**: Propose design mockups, technology stack comparisons (e.g., Next.js, Astro, Vite, Hugo), or feature concepts in [`ideas/idea-dump.md`](../ideas/idea-dump.md) or open an [Idea Proposal](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/idea_proposal.md).
2. **Discuss Architecture**: Join team discussions to agree on styling approaches, hosting (e.g., GitHub Pages, Vercel), and content management.
3. **Contribute**: Once specifications are agreed upon, open small, focused pull requests following the guidelines in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
