★ ACTS & POINT STRUCTURE

⚡ BUZZER & POUNCE PROTOCOL
  • Buzzer: First team to buzz gets the chance to answer. Arm/Disarm with Buzzer panel.
  • Pounce Window: Open pounce before reading answer. Correct = +10 pts, Incorrect = -5 pts penalty.
  • Auto-Grade: Click ⚡ Auto-Grade Matches to automatically score fuzzy-matched answers.
  • Doomsday Clock: Wrong buzzer attempts tick the Doom Clock +1 level closer to midnight (Level 12).
▶ TWO-STEP QUESTION NAVIGATION
  • Step 1 (First Click / N): Slams in the animated retro comic Answer Card with SFX on the projector. Button turns to Next: Advance Question ⏭.
  • Step 2 (Second Click / N): Advances to the next question with the 3D comic deck flip and resets the reveal state.
  • Manual Reveal: Click ★ Reveal Answer to reveal anytime without advancing.

  • N or → : Next (1st press = Reveal Answer, 2nd press = Advance Question)
  • P or ← : Previous Question
  • R : Reveal Answer on Display
  • H : Reveal Hint on Display
  • A : Toggle All-Rounds Master Answer Key
  • I : Toggle this Instructions & Format Guide
  • Esc : Close open drawers / modals
